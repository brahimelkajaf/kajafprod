Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

KAJAF Publisher is an internal tool run by KAJAF Prod on its own computer. It is used only by KAJAF Prod to publish KAJAF Prod's own videos to KAJAF Prod's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

What data the tool uses

  • Sign-in tokens issued by YouTube (Google), Meta and TikTok for KAJAF Prod's own accounts.
  • Basic account information (account or channel name and ID) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The videos, titles and captions KAJAF Prod creates.

How it is stored and shared

Tokens are stored only on KAJAF Prod's own computer. They are sent only to the platform that issued them, to upload videos. We do not collect data from other people, do not sell or share any data, and do not use it for advertising.

Deleting data

Access can be removed at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings; the tool's local tokens are then deleted. Questions: brahimelkajaf@gmail.com.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
